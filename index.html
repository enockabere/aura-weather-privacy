Privacy Policy for Aura Weather

Last updated: March 2026

Aura Weather is a simple weather application that provides weather forecasts using third-party weather services. Your privacy is important to us.

Information We Collect

Aura Weather does not collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Location Data

The application may request access to your device location if you choose to allow it. Location information is used only to provide weather information for your current area.

Your location data is not stored, recorded, or shared with the developer.

You can disable location access at any time from your device settings.

Third-Party Services

Aura Weather retrieves weather data from third-party weather APIs. These services may process your approximate location in order to return weather information.

Aura Weather does not control how third-party services handle data.

Data Storage

Aura Weather does not store personal user data on servers or databases.

Children’s Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
